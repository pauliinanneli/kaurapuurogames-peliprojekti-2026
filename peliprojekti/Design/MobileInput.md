#Mobile Input

Character can be moved at least up and down, MAYBE a little bit to the right and left but with a limited area for it.

Options would be maybe a drawn joystick on screen or using gestures (like swipes, drag-to-move) on screen to move character.

Gestures would maybe work better and be more intuitive if character would be moved mostly up and down on screen, joystick might be overkill for that.

Alternative could also be having arrows up and down on one side of screen for moving character up and down on the screen.

When in the end you make a constellation between stars to finish game, game uses dragging/swiping with finger.

Taps used to move from that constellation view to into the next "percent view" and taps also used in UI.

Nice to have would be adding an option in settings to choose joystick instead of gestures.
